Project Four

Libryx App

Libryx is a mobile app concept designed for people who want an easier way to read, exchange, and sell books without needing to visit a physical library. The app is useful for busy users and students who want access to books from home.

TypeGroup Project
My RoleUI Designer / UX Research Support
ToolsFigma, UX Research, UI Design, Moodboard, Navigation Design

Process & Reflection

How I approached the project.

Problem

Many people are busy and do not always have time to visit a library. Students may also want a simple way to exchange, sell, or discover books digitally.

Solution

Our team designed a mobile app where users can browse books, continue reading, track reading progress, exchange books, and explore book suggestions. I helped with UX research, moodboard direction, navigation ideas, and one of the main page layouts in Figma.

Reflection

This project helped me improve my Figma skills and understand how research supports better design decisions. I learned how clear navigation, readable layout, and simple user flow can make a mobile app easier to use.
